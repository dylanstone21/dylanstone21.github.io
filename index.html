About

Economics → Brand

People pay more for a name they trust. They stay loyal to products that aren't objectively better. That's the part of economics I couldn't stop thinking about.

I'm an economics student at the University of Michigan, graduating in May 2027, and I'm building a career in brand strategy. I came to branding through an interest in the psychology behind decision-making. Economics gave me models for how people should choose, but real decisions rarely follow them. The gap between the model and the real choice is brand, and it's what I want to work on.

My internships pulled me steadily closer to that question: from the entertainment touring industry, to brand consistency across 150+ car dealerships, to an employer brand in one of the most regulated industries there is.

When I'm not working, I'm making things: designs, videos, a running apparel brand of my own, and photos from wherever I've traveled.
